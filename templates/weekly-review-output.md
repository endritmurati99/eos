# EOS Weekly Review Output

## Purpose
Generate one combined Sunday `weekly_sync` message with a 7-day review and a concrete structural recommendation for the coming week.

## Required Input Pass
1. Review the last 7 Berlin-local days
2. Read stored daily evaluations when available
3. Read the coming week from Monday `00:00` to Monday `00:00`
4. Read open tasks if Google Tasks is available
5. Detect real patterns, overload, and repeated friction
6. Reduce flexible goals conservatively when the week is too full

## Output Format
### Rueckblick 7 Tage
- short recap of what actually shaped the last 7 days

### Muster
- real repeated signals such as overload, carry-over, fragmentation, or prep friction

### Naechste Woche hart gesetzt
- fixed commitments from the coming week

### Empfohlene Verteilung
- realistic placement of Deep Work, Gym, and Cardio

### Gekuerzte oder riskante Punkte
- reduced, deferred, or fragile goals with explicit reason

### Meine Empfehlung
- exactly 1 concrete recommendation for structuring the coming week

### Nicht vergessen
- prep points, transitions, and routine-critical reminders

## Rules
- the forward window is always the coming calendar week, never a rolling 7-day window
- the review window is the last 7 Berlin-local days
- if tasks are unavailable, state that limitation explicitly
- fixed work and fixed sport stay hard commitments
- use EOS reduction logic before inventing extra capacity
- keep the tone concise, operational, and non-generic
