# EOS Morning and Evening Booklet

Status: active iteration note

## Goal

Make EOS morning and evening messages feel like a small operational booklet: short, current, calendar-aware, and useful without becoming a generic productivity coach.

## Morning contract

The morning booklet should answer:

- What is fixed today?
- How many hard anchors, Deep Sessions, and reminders exist?
- What is the realistic focus?
- What is the minimum routine if the day is heavy?
- What must be carried/prepared?

Preferred shape:

```text
Fokus: ...
Kalender: X feste Termine, Y Deep-Sessions, Z Erinnerungen.
Rahmen: Start mit ...; Ende mit ...
Mitnehmen: ...
Minimum: ...
Risiko: ...
```

## Evening contract

The evening booklet should answer:

- What is fixed tomorrow?
- Which open loops matter for tomorrow?
- Is tomorrow Top-1 or Top-3 mode?
- What needs to be prepared tonight?
- What is the single recommendation?

Preferred shape:

```text
Stabil: ...
Offen: ...
Morgenkalender: X feste Termine, Y Deep-Sessions, Z Erinnerungen.
Rahmen: Start mit ...; Ende mit ...
Mitnehmen: ...
Energieverlust: ...
Morgen: ...
```

## Anti-goals

- No invented events, fake Top-3 priorities, or calendar guesses.
- No raw calendar dumps in chat.
- No motivational filler.
- No external writes without explicit approval.
